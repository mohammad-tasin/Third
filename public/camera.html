💡 Camera Tips
  • Hold the device steady to avoid motion blur
  • Ensure bright, even lighting on the chip surface
  • Position the chip text inside the blue guide frame
  • Try the torch button for better visibility in dim conditions
  • For best results, use a macro lens or get within 5–10cm of the chip
  • If no match found, try Upload mode with a higher-quality photo
